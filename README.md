CUBAFOOD.CA — MASTER WEBSITE BUILD



Build CUBAFOOD.CA as a world-class public platform for a real Canada–Cuba agricultural development, food sustainability, community-support and food-sovereignty initiative.



This website must be dramatically more substantial than a traditional nonprofit website.



It needs to function simultaneously as:



1. The public face of the agricultural project.

2. A recruitment platform for Cuban workers, farmers and volunteers.

3. A participation platform for Canadians and Cuban-Canadians.

4. A partnership portal for businesses, agricultural organizations and institutions.

5. A project-development journal.

6. A documentary/video platform.

7. A food and agricultural-support initiative.

8. A transparency portal.

9. A future donation and equipment-contribution platform.

10. A historical record showing the development of the project beginning in 2024.



The website must communicate one extremely clear idea:



WE ARE NOT ONLY TRYING TO SEND FOOD TO CUBA.



WE ARE TRYING TO HELP CUBA GROW MORE FOOD.



This is a long-term agricultural sustainability project connecting people and resources from Canada with Cuban farmers, workers, communities, agricultural organizations and local development.
